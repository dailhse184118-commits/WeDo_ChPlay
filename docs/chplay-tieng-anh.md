# Bản tiếng Anh cho trang CH Play (English (United States) – en-US)

Ngày 09/10/2026. Bản Android không bán gì trong app (gói trả phí chỉ có trên iPhone qua App Store và trên web), nên trang CH Play KHÔNG nhắc gói trả phí, giá hay cách thanh toán. Android không có "Đăng nhập bằng Apple".

## Cách thêm trên Play Console

1. Play Console → app WeDo → **Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing**.
2. Bấm **Manage translations → Add your own translations** (không chọn "Buy translations").
3. Tick **English (United States) – en-US** → **Add**.
4. Chọn en-US ở ô ngôn ngữ, dán các ô dưới, bấm **Save**. Ảnh chụp màn hình và đồ hoạ để trống thì Google dùng ảnh của ngôn ngữ mặc định (tiếng Việt).
5. Sửa trang chính không cần gửi bản build mới; Google duyệt thay đổi trang (thường vài giờ, tối đa vài ngày) — xem **Publishing overview → Send changes for review** nếu chế độ xuất bản có quản lý đang bật.

## App name (tối đa 30 ký tự)

```text
WeDo: Team Work for Students
```

## Short description (tối đa 80 ký tự)

```text
Group projects made easy: tasks, deadlines, project chat and meetings.
```

## Full description (tối đa 4000 ký tự)

```text
WeDo helps student teams run group assignments and projects with less chaos. Split work clearly, chat per project, and keep track of deadlines and meetings, all on your phone.

Think less, do more.

PROJECT CHAT
• Every project has its own chat room with real-time messages.
• Take a photo or pick one from your gallery to send; tap any image to view it full screen.
• Find projects quickly and see unread counts at a glance.

AI TASK SUGGESTIONS FROM MESSAGES
• A team Leader long-presses a message and chooses "Create task with AI". AI suggests a title, description, assignee and deadline.
• You review and edit before the task is created. Nothing is created automatically.
• Before first use, WeDo explains exactly what data is sent to the AI provider and only sends it with your consent.

DIRECT MESSAGES AND FRIENDS
• Chat one-on-one with teammates or friends, including photos.
• See who is online.
• Find classmates, send and answer friend requests.
• Report inappropriate messages and block people who bother you.

CLEAR TASKS
• See open tasks, tasks waiting for you and overdue tasks at a glance.
• Create tasks, set deadlines and pick an assignee. Project tasks are created by the team Leader.
• Accept a task, or decline it with a reason.
• Submit work with files. The Leader approves it, or returns it with a reason so you can fix it.

MEETINGS AND CALENDAR
• Leaders schedule meetings with a title, agenda, date, time and project.
• Join the online meeting room with one tap. The room opens in your browser.
• Read the meeting summary, decisions and action items. Minutes are created on the web version.
• The calendar groups deadlines, meetings and events by day.

TIMELY NOTIFICATIONS
• Get notified when you are assigned a task, when work is submitted or approved, and for new meetings, messages and friend requests.
• Deadline reminders 24 hours before and at the due time.
• Tap a notification to jump straight to the task, meeting or conversation.
• Choose which notifications you want: assignments, approvals, deadline reminders and meetings.

CONTRIBUTION BOARD
• See how many tasks each member has completed, what is still open or overdue, how much work they submitted and their on-time rate.

WORKSPACES AND ACCOUNT
• Create workspaces for your teams and switch between them.
• Sign in with Google, or with email and password.
• Change your avatar and update your name, phone number and date of birth.
• Send feedback to WeDo from inside the app.
• Delete your account yourself, right in the app.

WORKS WITH THE WEB VERSION
WeDo on Android shares your account and data with the WeDo web app. Projects and project members are created on the web, then the whole team works together on their phones. If you signed up with Google on the web, tap "Continue with Google" to reach the same account.
```
